# Alpha Surface

**Document ID:** `SPEC-005` **Status:** Implemented **Last Updated:** 2026-08-13 **Related:** [Strand Model](./strand-model.md), [CLI Surface](./cli.md), [REPL API](./repl-api.md), [Weaver Runtime](./daemon-runtime.md), [Spools index](../../spools/README.md), [Writing shared spools](../../docs/spools/writing-shared-spools.md)

## SPEC-005.P1 Purpose

This spec draws the line around what Millstrand ships as "alpha": which surface is in-contract (and where each contract lives), and which surface is explicitly internal. It is a contract index — it adds no behavior. The rule is exclusionary: surface not reachable through the in-contract tiers below is internal, regardless of Clojure var visibility or OS observability. TEN-000@1 still applies to everything: in-contract alpha surface evolves by accretion within a tier's own compatibility discipline, and internal surface may change without notice.

## SPEC-005.P2 In-contract surface

- **SPEC-005.C1:** The four root specs are the behavior contracts for shipped engine surface: strand model and storage semantics (SPEC-001), the public `strand`/`mill` CLI (SPEC-002), the trusted Clojure/REPL surface (SPEC-003), and the weaver runtime, transports, and registries (SPEC-004).
- **SPEC-005.C2:** The blessed spool-facing API is every `millstrand.api.*.alpha` namespace — currently `authoring`, `batch`, `cli`, `clock`, `current`, `errors`, `events`, `format`, `graph`, `hooks`, `lifecycle`, `notes`, `patterns`, `peers`, `registry`, `relations`, `return-shape`, `runtime`, `scheduler`, `millstrand`, `spec`, `spool`, `vocab`, `weaver` — plus `millstrand.test.alpha` and the human-facing `millstrand.repl` helpers. Each is specified in SPEC-003/SPEC-004 (errors in SPEC-003.C23d, relations in SPEC-001.P5, notes in SPEC-001, vocab in SPEC-001) and follows accretion-based compatibility within its subnamespace. `millstrand.api.return-shape.alpha` exposes the in-contract `validate!`, `explain`, and `check!` functions; `millstrand.test.alpha/check-op-return!` accretes within the already-listed author-side test tier.
- **SPEC-005.C3:** The reference spools shipped in the Millstrand tree — `batteries` and `unsafe-text-search` — are in-contract through their spool docs at [`spools/*.md`](../../spools/README.md), loaded **opt-in**: an approved `spools.edn` coordinate and a `:spools`-guarded `runtime/module!` declaration. Batteries follows the same path through the `{:millstrand/source-root "spools/batteries"}` entry that `mill init` seeds by default. Deleting that entry is its supported opt-out. No reference spool ships on the weaver classpath. `millstrand.spools.util` and `millstrand.spools.format` no longer name authoring helpers: `format` is deleted in favor of the already-blessed `millstrand.api.format.alpha` (`fill`, `reflow`), and `util` is promoted to blessed `millstrand.api.spool.alpha` (`fail!`, `reject-unknown-keys!`, `require-valid!`, `attr-key->str`, `attr-get`, `poll-until!`, `entity-projection`; SPEC-005.C2). The composable arg-spec fragments that once rode along (`note-surface`, `work-root`, `timeout-secs`, `outcome`) found no consumers and were removed before any release (PR #105); spool authors declare their own arg-spec data. `entity-projection` fails loudly unless its strand-shaped input contains `:id`, `:title`, `:state`, and `:attributes`, then returns exactly those four keys; domain-specific rows may extend that base explicitly, and richer existing rows are not narrowed. After this change the `millstrand.spools.*` family is exactly activatable spools and nothing else.
- **SPEC-005.C4:** Externally distributed domain spools (the Millhouse workflow, Chime, Cron, and gate-executor roots, plus the agent family in `codethread/agent-harness.spool`, devflow, and kanban) are userland, not shipped alpha surface. Their READMEs/docs are their own contracts with their own cadence, outside this line.
- **SPEC-005.C10:** The trained-vocabulary agent surface is frozen and does not follow the concept-naming rename: the `strand agent …` CLI verbs, the `agent-plan` weave pattern, the `agent-failures` named query, and the `:subagent` workflow-gate waiter value keep their current names. These are names agents and downstream configs are trained on, or whose blast radius is deliberately bounded (the `:subagent` waiter is pinned by `ct.spools.devflow`), so they stay put even as the spool namespaces and durable attribute keys beneath them move to concept-named vocabularies. The freeze covers exactly the tokens named here: flags, return keys, and attribute keys beneath a frozen verb are not frozen by this clause and follow SPEC-005.C11, as do the Clojure functions backing frozen verbs and the input field names of the frozen pattern.
- **SPEC-005.C11:** Spool vocabulary is inheritance-first: a spool that builds on a primitive — by invoking it or by reproducing its concept, whether the primitive is another spool, a blessed `millstrand.api.*.alpha` namespace, or a lower layer of the same spool — surfaces the primitive's published vocabulary (function verbs, op subcommands, flag names, return keys, attribute keys, phase values, edge relation names) and mints names only for concepts it introduces. The authoring statement, including the new-concept test, lives in [Writing shared spools](../../docs/spools/writing-shared-spools.md#the-rules-for-shared-spools); shipped and repo-local spools are held to it by review. Name freezes are spec decisions made in this document: SPEC-005.C10 is the only frozen surface, and a spool doc may not declare its own compatibility floor against convergence. Between peers (no invocation or reproduction relation in either direction), a shared concept converges on the word of the spool whose core purpose it is; dependency direction is depth, and peers filling one extension point name their surfaces in parallel.
- **SPEC-005.C12 (core authoring families):** `millstrand.api.millstrand.alpha` publishes three forms for each of the six core kinds: an inert `def<kind>` definition, a typed `use-<kind>!` selection, and a `def<kind>!` definition-and-selection shorthand. The kinds are `op`, `query`, `pattern`, `hook`, `handler`, and `bin`. `defbin` is the public definition form for the core `:bins` kind; its internal option grammar and declaration constructor remain in `millstrand.core.contribution`, while the effective registry and read protocol are owned by SPEC-004. The alpha index publishes the consulted `:millstrand.core.weaver.bins/list-result` and `:millstrand.core.weaver.bins/plan-result` result specs. `defbin` earns a public name under TEN-004 because `:bins` is Millstrand's own extension point beside the five core kinds. Making it a domain-spool kind would force every executable-shipping spool to depend on a deletable provider and would make the core `mill bin` family depend on that provider; core ownership keeps the shared extension point available without that module-ordering edge. PROP-Auf-001.AC1 requires a named form for a core kind, so once `:bins` is core, its three-form family is shipped API surface rather than workspace-local code.

## SPEC-005.P3 Explicitly internal

- **SPEC-005.C5:** `millstrand.core.*` is internal and may change freely (public vars included); trusted code that requires it accepts the compatibility cost (SPEC-004.C40).
- **SPEC-005.C5b:** `millstrand.api.*.internal` namespaces are implementation plumbing for their sibling `.alpha` modules (SPEC-003.C19a). They are not `millstrand.api.*.alpha` namespaces, so the exclusionary rule already places them outside the contract; like `millstrand.core.*`, their public vars may change freely.
- **SPEC-005.C5a:** Deterministic time is in-contract through the blessed tiers in C2: `millstrand.api.clock.alpha` owns the Clock abstraction, `millstrand.api.runtime.alpha/clock` and `now` expose the runtime Clock and its data-first read, and `millstrand.test.alpha/manual-clock`, `set-clock!`, and `advance!` are author-side controls (SPEC-004.C1a, SPEC-003.C17a/C28a). The runtime-map `:clock` slot and clock-pump registry remain internal core plumbing.
- **SPEC-005.C6:** The mill JSON socket protocol is internal transport glue between the shipped Go binaries: its frame shapes, operation set, `mill/*` error codes, protocol version, and the `mill.json` field set carry no contract. The contract is the CLI command surface (SPEC-002) plus the published artifact locations (SPEC-004.C9a). Nothing but the shipped binaries should dial `mill.sock`.
- **SPEC-005.C7:** On the weaver socket, the error `type` taxonomy and envelope shape are contract (SPEC-004.C24); concrete error `code` strings are contract only where individually specified (`operation/deadline-exceeded` SPEC-004.C26b, `query/not-found` SPEC-004.C36b, `hook/failed` SPEC-004.C84). Other code strings, and all human-readable message text, may change.
- **SPEC-005.C8:** Also internal: exact generated-file contents beyond their specified behavior (SPEC-002.C14a), client-side transport timeout values, storage mechanics beyond declared semantics (PRAGMAs, cascade mechanics, index choices), and the plain and pretty stderr rendering formats of error frames (plain's byte-faithful `details=` JSON payload stays contract per SPEC-002.C4). The `MILLSTRAND_ERROR_FORMAT=json` envelope is **not** internal: its one-line `type`/`code`/`message`/`details` shape is contract per SPEC-002.C4e, while the code strings inside it stay free to change per SPEC-005.C7.

## SPEC-005.P4 Change discipline

- **SPEC-005.C9:** Moving surface across the line — promoting internal surface into contract, or removing/reshaping in-contract surface — updates the owning root spec or spool doc, and this index when the tier membership itself changes. Internal-only changes need no spec update.
- **SPEC-005.C9a:** Deprecation in a `millstrand.api.*.alpha` namespace is advisory only. A deprecated var carries metadata `{:deprecated true :since "vN" :replacement fully-qualified-symbol}`; its behavior remains available forever under the published name. The first actual deprecation must also make this metadata visible in the generated API docs. Deprecation never makes a previously accepted call fail.
- **SPEC-005.C9b:** `millstrand.api.runtime.alpha` underwent a one-time in-place pre-v1 rewrite under TEN-000@1: its `sync!`/`use!`/global-`reload!` lifecycle was replaced by module declaration, live refresh, joined status, and the advanced code-only `reload-code!` seam (SPEC-003.P5, SPEC-004.C46), and the blessed per-entry registry mutation functions gained explicit owners (SPEC-003.C23). Removed vars received no deprecated aliases — the project has not reached Millstrand v1, and keeping both lifecycles would preserve the abstraction the rewrite exists to remove. `millstrand.api.registry.alpha` joined the blessed tier (SPEC-005.C2, SPEC-003.C23a) as the shared kind-declaration and owner-partition primitive; its core owner-registry implementation stays internal. This is a recorded exception to the C9a accretion default, not a repeal of it.
- **SPEC-005.C9c:** Under TEN-000@1 and explicit repository-owner approval, the core and lifecycle authoring families take one in-place, pre-v1 compatibility break: unbanged definition forms stop collecting, typed use forms select existing declarations, and bang forms define and select. Function-backed forms bind the exact supplied name. No deprecated alias, dual behavior, compatibility namespace, or feature probe ships. The coordinated userland migration is limited to Millhouse, agent-harness, devflow, and codethread; their own contracts and release markers record their corresponding breaks. Kanban v25 at peeled SHA `a6b3a36cd5476ec5c36cd58a7f74bfec6b7e665e` is the explicitly authorized external exception: this repository consumes only its Millstrand-owned core-only adapter surface under the fresh-generation constraint. Guild, peering operations, and lifecycle resources are absent. This clause makes no compatibility bridge or other compatibility claim for an external spool repository. The exception is bounded and does not repeal C9a's accretion discipline; another break needs another explicit root-spec decision.
